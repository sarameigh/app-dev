The Walking Dead (TV Series) Overview
The original series is a post-apocalyptic horror drama developed by Frank Darabont, based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman, Tony Moore, and Charlie Adlard.

Plot: The show takes place after the onset of a worldwide zombie apocalypse. The reanimated dead, known as "walkers," shamble toward living humans to eat them.

Initial Premise: The series begins with former sheriff's deputy Rick Grimes (played by Andrew Lincoln) waking from a coma to find the world overrun. He sets out to find his family and eventually becomes the leader of a growing group of survivors.


Core Theme: While facing the constant threat of walkers, the core drama centers on the interpersonal conflicts and the morality of the living. The biggest threats often come from other human survivors who have formed their own hostile groups and communities.

Seasons: The main series ran for 11 seasons (2010–2022).

Setting: It primarily takes place in and around Atlanta, Georgia, and later explores various safe zones and communities like the Alexandria Safe-Zone and the Hilltop Colony.
